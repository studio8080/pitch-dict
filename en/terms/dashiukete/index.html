📡Passer & Receiver

出し手と受け手

Passer & Receiver — The passer and the receiver. A pass is always a joint effort by two.

🇯🇵 この用語を日本語で読む：出し手と受け手 →

1💬 In a nutshell

“A misplaced pass isn't one person's fault — it's the pair's ‘timing.’”

2🎬 See it move

An original tactics board (blue = the team in focus, red = the opponent; attacking left to right). The board animates on its own.

3🔍 Why it matters — what to watch for

A good pass is the passer's technique × the receiver's movement × timing. Break it down — “the passer was early,” “the receiver wasn't ready” — and even a single mispass gains resolution. A basic analytical frame pundits lean on.

Analysts say“Wonderful movement from the receiver there — and the passer didn't miss it.”
