❤️‍🔥Vital Area

バイタルエリア

Vital Area — The danger zone in front of the box, between the DF and MF lines.

🇯🇵 この用語を日本語で読む：バイタルエリア →

1💬 In a nutshell

“vital = ‘life-threatening’ — literally the spot that leads straight to conceding.”

2🎬 See it move

An original tactics board (blue = the team in focus, red = the opponent; attacking left to right). The board animates on its own.

⚽ Open the interactive version (117 more terms, each with its own board)

3🔍 Why it matters — what to watch for

Let a player turn here and it's an unwinnable situation — a long shot, a killer pass or a through ball are all on. A defensive block's job, boiled down, is “don't leave the vital area open.”

On the call you'll hear“Turned in the vital area! Dangerous — and the shot's away!”
