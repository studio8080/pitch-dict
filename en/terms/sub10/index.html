🔁Sub 10s Rule

交代時の10秒ルール

Sub 10s Rule — A rule that a substituted player must leave promptly from the nearest line.

🇯🇵 この用語を日本語で読む：交代時の10秒ルール →

1💬 In a nutshell

“A system to stop the slow-walk time-wasting at subs.”

2🎬 See it move

An original tactics board (blue = the team in focus, red = the opponent; attacking left to right). The board animates on its own.

⚽ Open the interactive version (117 more terms, each with its own board)

3🔍 Why it matters — what to watch for

A measure against ‘slow substitutions’ late on. The outgoing player is prompted to exit quickly from the nearest touchline or goal line, and dawdling is cautionable — aimed at curbing the ballooning of added time.

On broadcasts“He's walking off slowly on purpose… that could even be a caution.”
