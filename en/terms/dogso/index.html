🟥Denying an Obvious Goal-Scoring Opportunity

DOGSO（ドグソ）

Denying an Obvious Goal-Scoring Opportunity — “Denying an obvious goal-scoring opportunity” — in principle a straight red.

🇯🇵 この用語を日本語で読む：DOGSO →

1💬 In a nutshell

“Kill a clear chance by fouling and you're off — instantly.”

2🎬 See it move

An original tactics board (blue = the team in focus, red = the opponent; attacking left to right). The board animates on its own.

⚽ Open the interactive version (117 more terms, each with its own board)

3🔍 Why it matters — what to watch for

Meet all four criteria — distance, direction, number of defenders, ball control — and it's DOGSO = straight red. Miss one and it's SPA (stopping a promising attack), a yellow. Now you can join the “is that a DOGSO?” debate.

On the call you'll hear“He's taken out the last man… possible DOGSO, VAR is checking.”
