▶️Advantage

アドバンテージ

Advantage — Not blowing for a foul when letting play go on favours the attacking side.

🇯🇵 この用語を日本語で読む：アドバンテージ →

1💬 In a nutshell

“Even on a foul, if playing on is better, the game isn't stopped.”

2🎬 See it move

An original tactics board (blue = the team in focus, red = the opponent; attacking left to right). The board animates on its own.

⚽ Open the interactive version (117 more terms, each with its own board)

3🔍 Why it matters — what to watch for

Sometimes stopping play actually rescues the fouling team. So the ref extends both arms — “play on!” He can also go back and show a card once the attack is over.

On the call you'll hear“He's fouled — advantage! Play carries on!”
