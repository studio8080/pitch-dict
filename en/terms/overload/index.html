🌊Overload

オーバーロード

Overload — Deliberately gathering players in one area to outnumber the opponent there.

🇯🇵 この用語を日本語で読む：オーバーロード →

1💬 In a nutshell

“Create a traffic jam on one side to leave the other wide open.”

2🎬 See it move

An original tactics board (blue = the team in focus, red = the opponent; attacking left to right). The board animates on its own.

⚽ Open the interactive version (117 more terms, each with its own board)

3🔍 Why it matters — what to watch for

Stack bodies and passes flow on the crowded side, drawing opponents in. Then switch it all to the far side, where it's a 1v1 or totally free. “Overload to isolate” is the golden pattern for unlocking a defence.

Columns put it“They compress the block with a right-side overload, leaving the far-side left winger isolated.”
