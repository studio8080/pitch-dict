🏟️Derby

ダービー

Derby — A match between rival clubs from the same city or region.

🇯🇵 この用語を日本語で読む：ダービー →

1💬 In a nutshell

“Standings don't matter — a grudge match you simply can't lose.”

2🎬 See it move

An original tactics board (blue = the team in focus, red = the opponent; attacking left to right). The board animates on its own.

⚽ Open the interactive version (117 more terms, each with its own board)

3🔍 Why it matters — what to watch for

A clash for a city's pride carries a special heat beyond table or talent. Manchester, Milan, Osaka, Saitama — famous derbies exist worldwide, and on this day players and fans become different people.

On the call you'll hear“It's a derby today. Forget the table — it's that kind of game.”
