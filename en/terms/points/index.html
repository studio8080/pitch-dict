🏆Points

勝ち点

Points — Win = 3, draw = 1, loss = 0 — the points that decide league standings.

🇯🇵 この用語を日本語で読む：勝ち点 →

1💬 In a nutshell

“The league's ‘currency’ — win 3, draw 1, lose nothing.”

2🎬 See it move

An original tactics board (blue = the team in focus, red = the opponent; attacking left to right). The board animates on its own.

⚽ Open the interactive version (117 more terms, each with its own board)

3🔍 Why it matters — what to watch for

Standings, survival and the title are all decided by this total. “Grabbing a point,” “a six-pointer” — late-season articles are all points maths. Wins were once worth 2, changed to 3 to encourage attacking football.

Analysts say“At this stage, taking a point without over-committing is a valid call too.”
