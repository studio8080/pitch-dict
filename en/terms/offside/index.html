🚩Offside

オフサイド（戻りオフサイド）

Offside — Receiving a pass after being beyond the second-last defender when it was played is an offence.

🇯🇵 この用語を日本語で読む：オフサイド →

1💬 In a nutshell

“Where you are ‘the instant the pass is played’ is everything — not when you receive it.”

2🎬 See it move

An original tactics board (blue = the team in focus, red = the opponent; attacking left to right). The board animates on its own.

3🔍 Why it matters — what to watch for

Judged on your position at the moment of the kick, so even if you drop back onside to receive it, you're still offside. And a late flag is deliberate — the official ‘delayed flag’ procedure — so hold the jeers.

On the call you'll hear“It's in — no, the flag's up! The run was just a fraction early.”
