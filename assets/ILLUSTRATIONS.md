# Affiliate category illustrations

Created with the built-in image generator on 2026-11-11 for this site. Three original illustrations show watching football, reading tactics books, and everyday football-inspired goods. They contain no advertiser logos and do not depict the actual products or services linked from the cards. Each card labels the illustration as an image.

The source PNGs are retained in the internal project deliverables; the site uses WebP exports at the original 1536 × 1024 dimensions. Affiliate URLs, third-party supplied HTML and tracking pixels are separate from these illustrations.
