🧲Compact / Stretched

コンパクト／間延び

Compact — Whether the shape is squeezed tight or stretched loose.

🇯🇵 この用語を日本語で読む：コンパクト →

1💬 In a nutshell

“A tight shape is a fortress; a stretched one is a house left open to burglars.”

2🎬 See it move

3🔍 Why it matters — what to watch for

The closer the front and back lines (compact), the fewer passing lanes the opponent finds. Conversely the gap between the lines the moment you stretch breeds chances. In a tiring second half, watch which team stretches first.

Analysts say“They're staying compact. The opponent's stretched a little, and the space between the lines is opening.”
