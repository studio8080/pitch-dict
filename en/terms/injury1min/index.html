⛑️1-Min Wait After Treatment

負傷治療後の1分待機

1-Min Wait After Treatment — A player treated on the pitch waits off it briefly before returning.

🇯🇵 この用語を日本語で読む：負傷治療後の1分待機 →

1💬 In a nutshell

“A temporary removal rule to reduce the ‘act-hurt’ ploy.”

2🎬 See it move

An original tactics board (blue = the team in focus, red = the opponent; attacking left to right). The board animates on its own.

⚽ Open the interactive version (117 more terms, each with its own board)

3🔍 Why it matters — what to watch for

A measure against feigning injury to waste time. After on-pitch treatment, the player must wait off the pitch for a set time (about a minute) before returning, cutting the payoff of going down easily (serious injuries and keepers are exceptions).
