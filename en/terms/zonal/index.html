🗺️Zonal / Man-marking

ゾーンディフェンス／マンツーマン

Zonal — “Guard a space” or “track a man” — the two main defensive systems.

🇯🇵 この用語を日本語で読む：ゾーンディフェンス →

1💬 In a nutshell

“A guard who patrols a zone, or a bodyguard glued to a VIP.”

2🎬 See it move

Man-marking: track the man
Zonal: guard the space

An original tactics board (blue = the team in focus, red = the opponent; attacking left to right). The board animates on its own.

3🔍 Why it matters — what to watch for

Zonal keeps the shape intact but is weak at hand-offs; man-marking is clear but risks being dragged around until the shape breaks. How a team defends set-pieces (zonal, man, or mixed) is a frequent talking point.

Analysts say“They defend corners zonally — a set-up with three at the near post.”
