🚚Conducción / Carry

コンドゥクシオン（キャリー／持ち運び）

Conducción — A centre-back or similar carrying the ball forward by dribbling.

🇯🇵 この用語を日本語で読む：コンドゥクシオン →

1💬 In a nutshell

“A dribble that breaks the defence by ‘carrying,’ not passing.”

2🎬 See it move

An original tactics board (blue = the team in focus, red = the opponent; attacking left to right). The board animates on its own.

⚽ Open the interactive version (117 more terms, each with its own board)

3🔍 Why it matters — what to watch for

When a CB carries, the opponent must have someone abandon their post to engage — and the instant they do, a team-mate comes free somewhere. Not a dribble to beat a man, but to create a gap. The new benchmark for a good CB.

Columns put it“His carrying drew out the midfielder and opened a passing route between the lines.”
