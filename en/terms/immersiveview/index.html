🎥Immersive View

イマーシブビュー

Immersive View — A new broadcast camera that puts you on the pitch from a player's eye level.

🇯🇵 この用語を日本語で読む：イマーシブビュー →

1💬 In a nutshell

“An ‘immersive’ feed like you're standing on the pitch.”

2🎬 See it move

An original tactics board (blue = the team in focus, red = the opponent; attacking left to right). The board animates on its own.

⚽ Open the interactive version (117 more terms, each with its own board)

3🔍 Why it matters — what to watch for

A talked-about new presentation this tournament. Low angles and CG put you into the players' eye level and around the ball, conveying the ‘distance’ and ‘pressure’ a bird's-eye view misses — thrilling even for newcomers.

On broadcasts“Now in immersive view — you can see the run in behind the defence from this close.”
