🪂Salida Lavolpiana

サリーダ・ラボルピアーナ

Salida Lavolpiana — A midfielder dropping between the CBs during build-up to make a back three.

🇯🇵 この用語を日本語で読む：サリーダ・ラボルピアーナ →

1💬 In a nutshell

“A ‘drop’ between the centre-backs — an emergency exit from the press.”

2🎬 See it move

An original tactics board (blue = the team in focus, red = the opponent; attacking left to right). The board animates on its own.

⚽ Open the interactive version (117 more terms, each with its own board)

3🔍 Why it matters — what to watch for

Against two pressing forwards, two CBs is a tough 2v2. Drop a midfielder in to make a 3v2 numbers-up and someone's always free. A world-standard build-up move popularised by Mexico's La Volpe.

Columns put it“The anchor's salida made a back three, letting them clear the first press line reliably.”
