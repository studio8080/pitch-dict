🎦Referee Cam

レフェリービュー

Referee Cam — Footage from a small camera worn by the referee.

🇯🇵 この用語を日本語で読む：レフェリービュー →

1💬 In a nutshell

“A new feed showing the referee's ‘eyes’ directly.”

2🎬 See it move

An original tactics board (blue = the team in focus, red = the opponent; attacking left to right). The board animates on its own.

⚽ Open the interactive version (117 more terms, each with its own board)

3🔍 Why it matters — what to watch for

An experiment using video from a small camera on the referee. It shows what the ref was seeing at the moment of a decision, adding weight to close calls. Together with VAR, it makes the ‘referee's viewpoint’ visible as never before.

On broadcasts“Referee view here — from the ref's position, this was the angle he had.”
