🛫Early Cross

アーリークロス

Early Cross — A cross played early rather than after reaching the byline.

🇯🇵 この用語を日本語で読む：アーリークロス →

1💬 In a nutshell

“A cross whipped in ‘before’ the defence gets set.”

2🎬 See it move

An original tactics board (blue = the team in focus, red = the opponent; attacking left to right). The board animates on its own.

⚽ Open the interactive version (117 more terms, each with its own board)

3🔍 Why it matters — what to watch for

Crosses from deep are potent, but the defence is home. An early cross forces defenders to deal with it running toward their own goal — attacker going forward vs defender back-pedalling: the advantage is obvious.

Analysts say“Whipped in early — defenders facing their own goal, the worst situation for them.”
