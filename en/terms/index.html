🎙 Live Commentary 56 terms

  • ⏳DelayディレイDefending by holding off instead of diving in, to buy time.
  • 🧱Low Blockブロックを敷く／ローブロックDropping into your own half and killing space with a wall-like shape.
  • 🎯The Pocket / Near Zoneポケットを突く（ニアゾーン）Running into the “corner” beside the penalty area.
  • 🚪Between the Linesライン間で受けるReceiving in the gap between the opponent's defensive and midfield lines.
  • 🛣️Channel Runチャンネル（チャンネルラン）Running the lane between the full-back and centre-back.
  • 🔥High Pressハイプレス／プレッシングWinning the ball high up as a group, deep in the opponent's half.
  • 🏰RetreatリトリートNot pressing, and dropping everyone back into your own half.
  • 🥊IsolationアイソレーションDeliberately clearing team-mates away to leave your star 1v1.
  • ✂️Cut InsideカットインDribbling diagonally inside from the wing toward the centre.
  • 🏃Overlap / Underlapオーバーラップ／インナーラップA player from behind running past the ball-carrier to go forward.
  • 🍀Second Ballセカンドボール（の回収）The loose ball that spills out after a duel.
  • 🧲Compact / Stretchedコンパクト／間延びWhether the shape is squeezed tight or stretched loose.
  • 🔨Vertical Pass into Feet楔のパス（くさびを入れる）A sharp vertical pass into a forward's feet.
  • 👥Cover ShadowカバーシャドウClosing down while using your back to block a passing lane.
  • 🪤Press Trap / Escapeはめる／はがすHerding into a defensive trap is ‘the trap’; escaping it is ‘breaking out.’
  • ⚡DuelデュエルA one-on-one contest for the ball.
  • 🔙Press BackプレスバックA forward pressing while running back toward his own goal.
  • 💨Run in Behind裏抜け（背後を取る）Breaking into the space behind the opponent's back line.
  • 🔮Through Passスルーパス（キラーパス）A pass into empty space for a runner to meet.
  • 🛫Early CrossアーリークロスA cross played early rather than after reaching the byline.
  • 💪IntensityインテンシティPlay intensity including duels, transitions and running.
  • 🪄FlickフリックA one-touch play that only changes the ball's direction.
  • 🤾Long Throw-inロングスローHurling a throw-in straight into the box as an attacking weapon.
  • 🌟GolazoゴラッソA super goal that makes you shout out loud.
  • 🪶Kamada's 1mm鎌田の1ミリThe equaliser vs the Netherlands at the 2026 WC that grazed Kamada's head by a millimetre.
  • 🤼Physical ContactフィジカルコンタクトBodying up. Fair shoulder-to-shoulder contact is part of football.
  • 📋Designed Set Pieceデザインされた（セットプレー）A free kick or corner broken down by a routine drilled in training.
  • ➡️Momentum Arrow（攻守の）矢印The direction a player's momentum and weight are pointing.
  • 🦅InterceptionインターセプトReading and stealing an opponent's pass in flight.
  • 🤲Trap / First TouchトラップThe skill of controlling an incoming ball into the next play.
  • 🤸Overhead / Bicycle Kickオーバーヘッド（バイシクル）Leaping to kick a ball higher than your head backward.
  • 🏋️Hold-up PlayポストプレーA forward receiving with their back to goal, shielding the ball from the defender and holding it until team-mates arrive.
  • 💥Long-range ShotミドルシュートA long-range shot from outside the penalty area.
  • 🏓One-Two / Wall Passワンツー（壁パス）A two-player combo: play it off a team-mate and take the return on the run.
  • 🛬Crossクロス（センタリング）A sideways or diagonal ball from wide into the box.
  • 🧭Near / Far Postニアサイド／ファーサイドThe side nearer the ball is the near post; the far side is the far post.
  • 🧹ClearanceクリアBooting a dangerous ball far away from in front of goal.
  • 🦸Super SubスーパーサブA trump-card substitute who changes the game off the bench.
  • ⏸️Holding the Ball溜め（ため）Holding the ball to make time and wait for team-mates to join.
  • ↔️Width / Stay Wide / Tuck In幅を取る（張る・絞る）Using the pitch's full width. Waiting out wide is ‘staying wide’; coming inside is ‘tucking in.’
  • 🔄Switch of PlayサイドチェンジA long pass that swings the ball to the opposite flank at once.
  • 🛟CoveringカバーリングDefence that instantly backs up behind a beaten team-mate.
  • 😏Malíciaマリーシア‘Cunning’ used to win within the rules.
  • 🧨Power PlayパワープレーAn all-out assault late on, sending tall players and even the keeper forward to lob it in.
  • 🦵Punt Kick / Feedパントキック／フィードA GK dropping the ball from his hands and kicking it long directly.
  • 🏟️DerbyダービーA match between rival clubs from the same city or region.
  • 🗡️Giant KillingジャイアントキリングA big upset where an underdog beats a powerhouse.
  • 🦾Sano Recovery佐野回収A phenomenon of hoovering up midfield loose balls at an absurd rate. From Kaishu Sano.
  • 📡Passer & Receiver出し手と受け手The passer and the receiver. A pass is always a joint effort by two.
  • 🔒Closing the Gameゲームをクローズする／クローザーEnding the match with the lead intact. The specialist is the ‘closer.’
  • 🚧Cutting the Passing Laneコースを切る（限定する）Blocking passing lanes with body angle and positioning to narrow the opponent's options.
  • 🛑Putting a Lid On蓋をするSealing off a dangerous route or player so it can't be used.
  • ↩️Cutbackカットバック（マイナスの折り返し）A last pass from near the goal line back away from goal.
  • ⚙️Shifting Gearsギアを上げるDeliberately raising the attacking tempo by a level.
  • 🎥Immersive ViewイマーシブビューA new broadcast camera that puts you on the pitch from a player's eye level.
  • ⚽adidas Match BalladidasボールThe official tournament ball. With a built-in sensor, it also aids precise decisions.

📝 Column & Analysis 33 terms

  • 💎Half-spaceハーフスペースThe zone “between centre and wing” when the pitch is split into five vertical lanes.
  • 🛤️Five Lanes5レーン理論Designing attacks by dividing the pitch into five vertical lanes.
  • ♟️Positional Play / Juego de PosiciónポジショナルプレーOut-doing opponents through positioning — designing “where to stand.”
  • 🎭False Nine / Zero Top偽9番（ゼロトップ）A centre-forward who drops from the front line into midfield.
  • 🕵️Inverted Full-back偽サイドバックA full-back who plays in central midfield rather than out wide.
  • 🚨Negative Transitionネガティブトランジション（ネガトラ）The moment of switching from attack to defence.
  • 🚀Positive Transitionポジティブトランジション（ポジトラ）The moment of switching from defence to attack.
  • 🌪️Gegenpressingゲーゲンプレス（カウンタープレス）Immediately swarming to win the ball back on the spot after losing it.
  • 🏗️Build-upビルドアップConstructing the attack by passing out from the GK and defenders.
  • 🌊OverloadオーバーロードDeliberately gathering players in one area to create a numbers-up.
  • 🤝Lay-offレイオフA forward who receives a vertical pass and knocks it short to a runner behind.
  • 👻Third Man Run3人目の動き（サードマン）A “third man,” not the passer or receiver, running in to collect.
  • ⚓Anchor / Pivoteアンカー／ピボーテA lone defensive midfielder stationed in front of the back line.
  • ⛈️StormingストーミングKick, recover, press instantly — a philosophy of creating storm-like chaos.
  • ➕Numerical / Positional / Qualitative Superiority数的優位／位置的優位／質的優位Being better off than the opponent in numbers, in position, or in individual quality.
  • 🗺️Zonal / Man-markingゾーンディフェンス／マンツーマン“Guard a space” or “track a man” — the two main defensive systems.
  • 📍Thirds of the Pitchアタッキングサード（ゾーン1・2・3）Splitting the pitch into three horizontal parts, numbered 1-2-3 from farthest to the attacker.
  • ❤️‍🔥Vital AreaバイタルエリアThe danger zone in front of the box, between the DF and MF lines.
  • 🛡️Mid BlockミドルブロックSetting the defensive block at mid height, in the middle third.
  • 🚚Conducción / Carryコンドゥクシオン（キャリー／持ち運び）A centre-back or similar carrying the ball forward by dribbling.
  • 🪂Salida Lavolpianaサリーダ・ラボルピアーナA midfielder dropping between the CBs during build-up to make a back three.
  • 📐Diagonal RunダイアゴナルランA run cutting across the pitch on the diagonal.
  • 📌Pinningピン留めFixing an opponent in place through positioning alone.
  • 📏High LineハイラインA defensive setting that keeps the last line high up.
  • ⚔️Counter Attackカウンター（ショート／ロング）A fast attack right after winning the ball, before the opponent gets set.
  • 🚗VolanteボランチA defensive midfielder steering both phases from the base of midfield.
  • 🌒Shadow Strikerシャドー（セカンドトップ）An attacker just behind the CF — the ‘2’ in a 3-4-2-1.
  • 🌘False Shadow偽シャドーA variable where the shadow leaves his spot to drop into midfield and build.
  • 🧰Polyvalent / UtilityポリバレントThe versatility to play several positions at a high level.
  • 👑The Ace as a Tactic戦術三笘（戦術ヴィニシウス）Making ‘let the ace go 1v1’ itself a team rule.
  • 🧠Football IQサッカーIQThe ‘mental speed’ to read a situation, predict, and choose the best option.
  • 🦎Fluid System可変システムA setup that reshapes the formation between attacking and defending phases.
  • 🐟Baiting the Defender食いつかせるDeliberately drawing a defender out to use the space he leaves.

📊 Data & Stats 13 terms

  • 🎲Expected GoalsxG（ゴール期待値）A 0–1 figure for the chance a given shot is scored.
  • 🎁Expected AssistsxA（アシスト期待値）A figure for how likely a pass was to lead to a goal.
  • 🧮Passes Per Defensive ActionPPDAHow many opponent passes are allowed per one defensive action.
  • 🌡️Heat MapヒートマップA colour-shaded map of where a player spent the most time.
  • 🕸️Pass Networkパスネットワーク図A diagram of who passed to whom, shown by line thickness.
  • ⏱️Possessionポゼッション（ボール支配率）The share of match time a team held the ball.
  • 📈Progressive PassプログレッシブパスA pass that advances the ball significantly toward goal.
  • 🎚️Field TiltフィールドティルトShare of play in the opponent's final third — who was pinning who back.
  • 🅰️AssistアシストThe record for the pass ‘one before’ a goal.
  • 🎩Hat-trickハットトリックOne player scoring three goals in a single match.
  • 🧤Clean SheetクリーンシートFinishing a match without conceding. A medal for the GK and defence.
  • 🏆Points勝ち点Win = 3, draw = 1, loss = 0 — the points that decide league standings.
  • ⏲️Actual Playing TimeAPT（アクチュアルプレーイングタイム）The net time the ball was actually in play out of the 90 minutes.

⚖️ Rules & Referee 16 terms

  • 🚩Offsideオフサイド（戻りオフサイド）Being ahead of the second-last defender when the pass is played is an offence.
  • 🎣Offside TrapオフサイドトラップDeliberately stepping the DF line up to catch a forward offside.
  • 🟥Denying an Obvious Goal-Scoring OpportunityDOGSO（ドグソ）“Denying an obvious goal-scoring opportunity” — in principle a straight red.
  • 📺Video Assistant Referee / On-Field ReviewVAR／OFRAn assistant referee using video, and the pitch-side monitor review.
  • ▶️AdvantageアドバンテージNot blowing for a foul when letting play go on favours the attacking side.
  • 🔬Mitoma's 1mm三笘の1ミリThe historic 2022 WC vs Spain call where the ball stayed on the line by just 1.88mm.
  • 🥤Hydration Break飲水タイム（ハイドレーションブレイク）A roughly one-minute drinks break once per half in extreme heat.
  • ⌛Additional TimeアディショナルタイムTime added at the end for stoppages. Formerly ‘loss time.’
  • ✋Handballハンド（ハンドリング）An offence of handling the ball with hand or arm — but not ‘touch = automatic foul.’
  • 🥅Penalty / Free KicksPK／直接FK・間接FKRestart variations that change by the place and type of foul.
  • 🤥Simulation / Diveシミュレーション（ダイブ）Falling with no contact to deceive the referee. A cautionable offence.
  • 😱Own Goalオウンゴール（OG）Putting the ball into your own net.
  • 🎦Referee CamレフェリービューFootage from a small camera worn by the referee.
  • ⏱️5-Second Countdown5秒カウントダウンA finger countdown the ref shows when a keeper holds the ball too long.
  • 🔁Sub 10s Rule交代時の10秒ルールA rule that a substituted player must leave promptly from the nearest line.
  • ⛑️1-Min Wait After Treatment負傷治療後の1分待機A player treated on the pitch waits off it briefly before returning.
