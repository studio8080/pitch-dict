🛡️Mid Block

ミドルブロック

Mid Block — Setting the defensive block at mid height, in the middle third.

🇯🇵 この用語を日本語で読む：ミドルブロック →

1💬 In a nutshell

“Neither charging up nor shutting yourself in — a middle stance.”

2🎬 See it move

An original tactics board (blue = the team in focus, red = the opponent; attacking left to right). The board animates on its own.

⚽ Open the interactive version (117 more terms, each with its own board)

3🔍 Why it matters — what to watch for

A high press drains the legs, a low block gets pinned back. The mid-block balances economy and aggression: “fine to let them have it this far — bite once they step in.” Read the three line heights and the manager's intent shows.

Analysts say“A mid-block today — not chasing needlessly, luring them into midfield before pouncing.”
