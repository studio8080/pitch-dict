⏱️5-Second Countdown

5秒カウントダウン

5-Second Countdown — A finger countdown the ref shows when a keeper holds the ball too long.

🇯🇵 この用語を日本語で読む：5秒カウントダウン →

1💬 In a nutshell

“A visible ‘hurry up’ — a countdown aimed at the keeper.”

2🎬 See it move

An original tactics board (blue = the team in focus, red = the opponent; attacking left to right). The board animates on its own.

⚽ Open the interactive version (117 more terms, each with its own board)

3🔍 Why it matters — what to watch for

A new operation against keeper time-wasting. Hold it too long and the ref raises a hand and counts down the last 5 seconds on his fingers; exceed it and it's the opponent's ball. Time-wasting got harder and the tempo went up.

On broadcasts“The ref's started the count! 5, 4… keeper, release it quick!”
