🕸️Pass Network

パスネットワーク図

Pass Network — A diagram of who passed to whom, shown by line thickness.

🇯🇵 この用語を日本語で読む：パスネットワーク図 →

1💬 In a nutshell

“The team's ‘friendship chart’ — thick lines are the attacking arteries.”

2🎬 See it move

An original tactics board (blue = the team in focus, red = the opponent; attacking left to right). The board animates on its own.

⚽ Open the interactive version (117 more terms, each with its own board)

3🔍 Why it matters — what to watch for

Thick lines = that team's attacking routes, showing which players the attack runs through. “Thin lines to the anchor” means he's being shut down. A regular in social-media match reviews.

Analysis put it“The pass network shows the attack skewed heavily left, with the right winger almost isolated.”
