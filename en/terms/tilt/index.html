🎚️Field Tilt

フィールドティルト

Field Tilt — Share of play in the opponent's final third — who was pinning who back.

🇯🇵 この用語を日本語で読む：フィールドティルト →

1💬 In a nutshell

“The ‘tilt’ of the match — in whose half was football being played.”

2🎬 See it move

An original tactics board (blue = the team in focus, red = the opponent; attacking left to right). The board animates on its own.

⚽ Open the interactive version (117 more terms, each with its own board)

3🔍 Why it matters — what to watch for

A next-gen stat said to reflect reality better than possession. Idly passing in your own half doesn't raise it — only time spent pinning them back counts. “Possession level but 70% tilt” is effectively one-sided.

Articles put it“Field tilt 74%. The score is 1-0, but in substance it was a rout.”
