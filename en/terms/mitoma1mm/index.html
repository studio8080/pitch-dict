🔬Mitoma's 1mm

三笘の1ミリ

Mitoma's 1mm — The historic 2022 WC vs Spain call where the ball stayed on the line by just 1.88mm.

🇯🇵 この用語を日本語で読む：三笘の1ミリ →

1💬 In a nutshell

“The 1.88mm that taught the world: it's not out until it's ‘fully’ over.”

2🎬 See it move

An original tactics board (blue = the team in focus, red = the opponent; attacking left to right). The board animates on its own.

⚽ Open the interactive version (117 more terms, each with its own board)

3🔍 Why it matters — what to watch for

From the side it's clearly out. But from directly above, part of the ball was still over the goal line — by 1.88mm. Kaoru Mitoma's cut-back set up Ao Tanaka's winner, and Japan beat Germany and Spain to top the group. A legendary “on the line is in” call of the VAR era.

Still used today“Tight, but maybe it's still in, Mitoma's-1mm style? Thank god for VAR.”
