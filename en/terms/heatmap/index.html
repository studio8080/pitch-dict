🌡️Heat Map

ヒートマップ

Heat Map — A colour-shaded map of where a player spent the most time.

🇯🇵 この用語を日本語で読む：ヒートマップ →

1💬 In a nutshell

“See a player's range like a thermal image.”

2🎬 See it move

An original tactics board (blue = the team in focus, red = the opponent; attacking left to right). The board animates on its own.

⚽ Open the interactive version (117 more terms, each with its own board)

3🔍 Why it matters — what to watch for

A right-back glowing red centrally = inverted full-back; a CF red in midfield = false nine — the role's trick is exposed at a glance. When it appears in half-time stats, compare it with the listed position.

On broadcasts you'll see“Look at his heat map — listed as a left winger, but actually playing centrally.”
