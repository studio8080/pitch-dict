🎯The Pocket / Near Zone

ポケットを突く（ニアゾーン）

The Pocket — Running into the “corner” of the penalty area, wide of the six-yard box.

🇯🇵 この用語を日本語で読む：ポケットを突く →

1💬 In a nutshell

“Next to the prime real estate in front of goal: an empty plot in the blind spot.”

2🎬 See it move

An original tactics board (blue = the team in focus, red = the opponent; attacking left to right). The board animates on its own.

⚽ Open the interactive version (117 more terms, each with its own board)

3🔍 Why it matters — what to watch for

Take the pocket and you can play a cut-back (a final pass pulled back, away from goal), one of the deadliest scoring patterns in modern football. Right after “they've taken the pocket!” a goal often follows — don't look away.

On the call you'll hear“Into the pocket! The cut-back — buried it!”
